What is a stock?

Think of it like owning a pizza slice

Imagine your favorite pizza place wants to expand. They split the business into 100 slices and sell each for $10. You buy 1 slice — you now own 1% of the pizza shop.

That slice is a stock. If the shop becomes worth $2,000, your slice is now $20. If it drops to $500, your slice is $5.

💡 Key idea

When a company does well its price goes up — which is why researching before investing matters.
